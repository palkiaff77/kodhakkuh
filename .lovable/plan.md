

# 🌌 The Proposal Universe — For Yazhini

A cinematic, romantic proposal website that transforms a simple QR scan into an unforgettable experience. Every page is designed to build emotion, from the first glowing particle to the final love portal.

---

## 📁 Project Structure — Easy Media Swapping
A dedicated `public/media/` folder organized into:
- **`media/pictures/`** — 15 placeholder images for the memory cinema
- **`media/videos/`** — Placeholder proposal video
- **`media/audio/`** — Background music, voice notes (6 placeholders), sound effects (heartbeat, tick, whisper)

All file references will be clearly labeled in code so you can simply swap files by name.

---

## 🎬 Scene 1 — Time-Aware Entry + Anniversary Lock
- **Black screen** → a single glowing particle expands into an animated starfield (2D canvas, smooth and fast)
- **Time-based greeting**: Morning gets golden warm tones with "Good morning, Yazhini ☀️", Night gets deep blue stars with "Good evening, Yazhini 🌙". Special 11:11 shooting star + "Make a wish. I already did."
- **Glowing heart** appears — "Touch my heart" — tapping it shatters into particles
- **Anniversary Date Lock**: 3 smooth scrolling wheels (DD / MM / YYYY) with soft tick sounds and particle reactions
  - ✅ Correct (07/01/2026): Pink-gold glow, heartbeat sound, vibration, galaxy zoom transition
  - ❌ Wrong: Gentle shake, "That's not when destiny started…"
- **Every refresh / QR scan always returns here** — no state is saved

---

## 🎙 Scene 2 — Voice Unlock
- Minimal glowing background with pulsing microphone icon
- "Say 'I love you'… and I'll show you what my heart has been waiting to say."
- **Browser Speech Recognition** listens for variations: "i love you", "love you", "i love u", "love u", "i lov u", etc.
- On detection: sound wave animation, screen glows pink, whisper audio "I love you more, Yazhini", particle burst transition
- **Skip button** always visible (subtle) for fallback if mic doesn't work

---

## ⏳ Scene 3 — 30-Second Memory Cinema
- Cinematic countdown 0→30 with the **counter styled subtly** so photos are the star
- 15 placeholder photos float in as glass-like panels with slow parallax
- 4 short video clip placeholders loop between photos
- Overlay quotes fade in/out: "The day you walked into my life…", "The smile that ruined my loneliness…", etc.
- Soft instrumental builds gradually
- At 27s: music intensifies, particles swirl faster
- At 29s: heartbeat sound
- At 30: everything freezes → 1 second silence → fade to black

---

## 🎬 Scene 4 — Proposal Video
- Elegant glowing button: "If your heart feels it… press this."
- Small subtle "(Skip)" text below — always present so she can skip on future visits
- Tapping plays the placeholder proposal video fullscreen
- **Skip button visible during video playback** to jump ahead on repeat visits
- After video ends → transition to Forever Love Portal

- Near video end: glowing golden ring forms from particles, rotates slowly, then fades

## 🌌 Scene 5 — Forever Love Portal
- Warm cosmic background with floating particles and ambient music
- "Whenever you miss me, Yazhini… this place will always bring me back to you."
- **Four glowing orbs** appear, each pulsing gently:

### 💌 Orb 1 — Letters From My Heart
- Random love letter each visit (20 pre-written letters)
- Beautiful paper-unfold animation on glass card
- Different letter every click

### 🎧 Orb 2 — Hear My Voice
- 6 placeholder voice notes: "Did you eat?", "I'm proud of you.", "Smile for me.", "I miss you.", "Come here.", "Sleep peacefully."
- Circular waveform animation while playing
- Background dims with soft glow — stored in `media/audio/` for easy swapping

### 🌌 Orb 3 — Feel My Hug
- Screen fills with warm golden light
- Phone vibrates gently
- "Close your eyes for 5 seconds, Yazhini." → slow pulse timer
- "That was me holding you."

### 💬 Orb 4 — Talk To Me
- Elegant minimal chat UI with typing animation
- 40+ pre-programmed romantic keyword-matched responses
- Examples: "Are you there?" → "Always." / "Do you love me?" → "More than yesterday. Less than tomorrow."

## ✨ Design Philosophy
- **Fonts**: Elegant serif for headings, clean sans-serif for body — large, visible, and attractive
- **Colors**: Deep cosmic blues, warm golds, soft pinks, glowing whites
- **Animations**: Every transition is cinematic — fade, particle burst, glass panel float
- **Mobile-first**: Designed for phone scanning via QR code
- **Always fresh**: Every refresh/scan restarts from Scene 1 (the date password page)
